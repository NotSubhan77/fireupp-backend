Zovio Chats

End-to-end encrypted direct & group messaging. Send encrypted voice notes, view-once photos/videos, pin critical messages, react with emoji, and edit sent messages up to 15 minutes after sending.

E2E Encrypted Voice Notes 15-Min Edit Live Translator

Zovio Calls & Meetings

Unified 1-on-1 audio/video calling, group calls, and room meetings using join codes. Built on ultra-low latency WebRTC P2P with hardware DSP enhancement and background noise cancellation.

Native Screen Sharing

Discord-inspired native window & full display selector. Stream your desktop or specific applications with crisp 60 FPS video and clean system audio streaming for presentations or co-op gaming.
