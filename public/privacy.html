Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Developer Contact: notsubhan777@gmail.com

Zovio ("we", "our", or "the App") is a zero-knowledge, end-to-end encrypted messaging, calling, and workspace application. This Privacy Policy explains how data is processed when you use Zovio on desktop or web.

1. End-to-End Encrypted Chats & Workspace Data

  • Messages, shared media, ZovioSpace notes, calendar events, tasks, and whiteboards are encrypted client-side before leaving your device.
  • Temporary encrypted relay storage (Backblaze B2 / Appwrite) is used solely to deliver media between offline devices and is automatically removed once delivered after the configured relay window.

2. Google Account & Google Drive Backup Data

When you optionally connect your Google Account in Zovio:

  • Authentication: If you sign in with Google, Zovio receives your basic profile (name, email address, and profile avatar) solely to create and authenticate your Zovio account.
  • Google Drive App Folder (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata): If you enable Google Drive Backup, Zovio requests access strictly to the hidden application-specific folder (appDataFolder) inside your personal Google Drive. Zovio cannot see, read, modify, or delete any other files in your Google Drive.
  • All chat snapshots, workspace items, and cached files backed up to your Drive are encrypted client-side with your personal Zovio vault key before upload. Tokens remain on your device and are never transmitted to Zovio servers.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use)

Zovio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs strictly adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Zovio never sells Google user data, never uses it for advertising, and never transfers it to third-party AI/ML training or data brokers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You may disconnect Google Drive at any time in Settings → Security & E2EE, remove Zovio's access from your Google Account permissions, clear your local device cache, or permanently delete your Zovio account under Settings → Delete Account.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data deletion requests, contact notsubhan777@gmail.com.
